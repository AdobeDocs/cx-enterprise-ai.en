# Coming soon: Default workspaces with cross-team campaign visibility {#new-teams-experience}

## Action may be required: A new Teams experience is coming October 15, 2026

### What's changing and what will it impact?

From October 14, 10 p.m. PDT to October 16, 6 a.m. PDT, we will be making critical product updates to introduce initial team capabilities, making it easier for teammates to view each other's campaign boards.

This update requires a fresh start, which means your **existing conversations, campaigns, and data will not carry over** to the new experience.

Access to the app will be temporarily unavailable during this window and will resume as soon as the upgrade is complete.

### How to save your work

To keep anything for your records, save it locally before October 14, 10 p.m. PDT:

* **Prompts**: Copy any important prompts using the copy icon below a message, and save them locally.
* **Campaigns**: Export each campaign as a PDF or Word file using the download icon in the top-right corner of the campaign.
* **Emails**: Download an email as HTML using the export icon in the email editor.
