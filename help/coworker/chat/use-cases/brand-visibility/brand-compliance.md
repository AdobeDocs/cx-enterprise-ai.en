# Check content for brand compliance with Coworker Chat

Coworker simplifies maintaining brand consistency in your marketing workflows. It integrates seamlessly to evaluate content against established brand guidelines.

For example, Coworker can review automatically generated content, like an Instagram caption, to ensure it aligns with your brand. It performs checks using AEM governance features and other tools, providing results that highlight passed, failed, and non-applicable items.

Coworker doesn't just identify issues; it offers actionable suggestions. For instance, if a caption is too generic or lacks a call to action, it provides a revised version that meets your brand standards, complete with relevant tags. This process streamlines content creation and ensures brand compliance with minimal effort.
