# Generate marketing assets with Coworker Chat

Coworker simplifies the process of creating brand assets for social media and other channels. Instead of managing each task manually, you can describe your goal, and Coworker handles the steps to achieve it.

For example, to create an Instagram coffee post, you can provide a brief idea, and Coworker will suggest concepts and generate optimized content. It selects suitable images, adjusts dimensions, and formats them for the platform, saving time and ensuring quality.

Coworker also offers flexibility for further customization. You can modify assets, create variations, or adapt content for different formats, such as Instagram stories. This approach allows you to focus on your goals while Coworker manages the technical details.
